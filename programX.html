PROGRAM X

Dive deep into the soundscapes of the future. Program X is WUSB's premier outlet for retro-futurism, vintage synth grooves, and vaporwave sound collages. Broadcasting from the heart of campus, host DJ Void blends driving 80s basslines, analog drum machines, and ambient textures for a two-hour sonic voyage.

First broadcast in 2024, Program X remains an essential late-night companion for electronic enthusiasts worldwide.

Episode 45: Retro-Future Beats & Tape Loops

Broadcast Date: June 25, 2026

Featured Log

On tonight's episode of Program X, host DJ Void took listeners on an analog odyssey. We explored the unique warm textures of late-80s tape loops, featured recent cassettes from local synth labels, and showcased modular synth patches crafted live in the studio.
