WUSB Resident DJ

DJx

DJx is a resident sound designer and radio host broadcasting live from Stony Brook University. Known for blending classic analog frequencies with forward-thinking cyber beats, DJx crafts immersive auditory journeys that cross the boundaries of synthwave, ambient soundscapes, and high-energy vapor-techno.

With over a decade of crate-digging experience, DJx’s weekly air blocks serve as a curated exhibition of rare cassette releases, live modular hardware patches, and exclusive local music showcases. Whether you are driving down a midnight highway or coding in the dark, DJx provides the ultimate soundtrack.
